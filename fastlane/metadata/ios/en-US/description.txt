Astro calculates your natal chart from the date, time and place of birth and offers an interpretation for every element of it. Everything runs on your phone: no account, no ads, no internet connection.

The app's interface and all interpretations are in Czech.

CHART
• chart wheel with planets, angles and aspects, aspect patterns and 32 fixed stars
• tables of positions, houses and aspects; nine house systems and a comparison of them
• interpretations of planets in signs and houses, aspects, angles and more
• birth time unknown? The chart is calculated without houses and angles

TRANSITS AND TIME
• today's transits, the day hour by hour with the Moon and planetary hours, a calendar
• secondary progressions and solar returns

RELATIONSHIPS
• synastry of two profiles: relationship guide, periods, combined wheel, tables and interpretation
• partners, parent and child, siblings, friends and colleagues
• saved pairs and sharing the reading as a PDF

ASK
• what in your chart explains a trait, and what was going on on a given day
• horary questions judged by traditional 17th-century rules
• electional search for a suitable time; windows can be added to your calendar

PREDICTION JOURNAL
• rate your days and check whether transit tension relates to how you actually feel
• optional blind test, control against someone else's chart, and your own hypotheses
• evening reminder as a local notification

NOTABLE PEOPLE
• a catalogue of nearly 300 notable people with birth data from Wikidata; birth time only where a source documents it

DIFFICULT TOPICS UNDER YOUR CONTROL
The readings also cover shadow sides and difficult topics that traditional astrology writes about: illness, loss, crisis. Gentle mode, or hiding individual topics, can be switched on at any time in the settings.

PRIVACY
Profiles, the journal and questions stay on your phone, stored encrypted. The app sends nothing and collects no data.

Planet positions are calculated from VSOP87 and Meeus, Pluto from the JPL DE440 ephemeris.

Astro describes the traditional interpretation of astrology, not proven connections. It is neither a prediction nor advice, and it does not replace medical or psychological help.
