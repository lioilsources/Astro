Astro: horoskop is an offline astrology app in Czech (the interface and all texts are Czech only). It calculates a natal chart from the date, time and place of birth and shows a written interpretation of each element, plus transits, progressions, solar returns, synastry of two profiles, horary questions, an electional search and a prediction journal. There is no account, login, chat or user-generated content shared with anyone.

How to use: on first launch the app explains that the readings include difficult topics and offers a gentle mode (either choice works). Profiles tab > "Přidat profil" (Add profile): enter a name, birth date, time and place (search e.g. "Praha"), save. Tap the profile to open the chart: tabs Přehled (wheel), Tabulky (tables), Výklad (reading), Tranzity (transits). With two profiles, Profiles > Vztahy > "Přidat vztah" (Add relationship) opens synastry. Other tabs: Osobnosti (catalogue of notable people), Zeptej se (ask / horary / election), Slovníček (glossary), Nastavení (settings, including content filter).

Content: the readings describe the traditional interpretation of astrology and say so in the app; they are not presented as predictions or advice. Because traditional astrology also writes about illness, loss, death and crises, the app asks on first launch whether to show these passages and lets the user hide each topic in Settings; next to hidden passages it shows the Czech crisis line 116 123.

Data: everything is calculated on the device. Profiles, the journal and horary questions are stored only on the device, encrypted (AES-GCM, key in the Keychain). The app makes no network requests: no servers, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs. Optional local notifications are used only for the evening journal reminder. "Share" creates a PDF or calendar file and hands it to the system share sheet.

No in-app purchases. No regulated industry. Third-party data: place names from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), birth data of notable people from Wikidata (CC0) with short attributed Wikipedia quotes for event dates, Noto fonts (SIL OFL); all credited in Nastavení > O appce a zdrojích.
